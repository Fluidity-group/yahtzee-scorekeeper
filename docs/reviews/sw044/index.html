Not just a colour change

Three different
ways to feel the game.

Ordinary score: a short settle and total update.

Bonus: the target resolves, with a separate +35 beat.

Lead change: a stronger graphic cue, once.

Title secured: a brief shared moment, followed by the next action.

Touch stays live throughout. No waiting for effects. Reduced motion keeps every fact; sounds are short, optional local effects. There is no speech.

Build for the two people playing

A short path back to your phones.

01

Choose the feel.

Rally is my recommended base. Borrow Night Match’s race lanes and Playform’s bonus animation. One coherent game, not three themes to maintain.

02

Get iOS playing.

Use the available Mac to build the existing manual game first. Test Amber hosting and Keith joining, then reverse it. Keep the Home Screen web app as a fast fallback.

03

Ship one lovely turn.

Bring the chosen scoring slice to Android and iOS together. Same rules, accounts, tournament and Undo. No voice work or engine rewrite as a dependency.

04

Play, measure, grow.

Polish the race, stats and motion from complete games together. Clear build IDs, easy returns and useful diagnostics. Commercial decisions can wait.

Mac available is confirmed. Xcode, signing and the first iPhone build still need checking. No iOS build, APK update or live tournament change is part of this design lab.
