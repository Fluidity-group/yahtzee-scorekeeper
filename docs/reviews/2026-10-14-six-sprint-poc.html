The experience we are aiming for

Your frame comes first

Centered existing-style score dialogs with large count buttons or a keypad. Own scores stay prominent; smaller anonymous opponents appear immediately to their right on matching category rows.

Energy with a purpose

Premium mint/violet color, satisfying score landings and category signatures. Drawers hold table details and options. Reduced motion preserves the facts; scoring never waits for a celebration.

A commentator with memory

Original dry, sharp, sometimes dark comedy from accepted facts. Independent profanity/intensity/frequency controls, ElevenLabs Android speech, shared captions and audience-aware freshness.

SW-019 has recorded qualification and rollout evidence, but real return/Undo and cached-client activation remain open. SW-021 commentary is researched design, not a working current-game integration. The updated UI is a local design demo, not connected multiplayer.

The six-sprint delivery path

01

Agree the contracts. Recover the baseline.

A clear phone flow and a reliable foundation for parallel implementation.

UX specialistAgree centered modal, anonymous same-row peers, drawers and premium motion defaults.
Multiplayer / reliabilityReconcile source baseline; close or reproduce current Undo, paused return, paging and cached-update gaps.
Commentary specialistSynthetic repeated-game replay; accepted facts, versioned settings and exposure/memory contracts.
Exit gate: identified source, reviewable contracts, named owners and measurable POC criteria. Resolve budget, retention and label policy before dependent execution. Synthetic evidence does not establish physical acceptance.
02

Build touch UX. Audition the voice.

Comfortable scoring, expressive category moments and a commentator chosen by listening.

Touch / UXProduction centered count/fixed/keypad dialogs, read-only B/C/D… peers, synchronized rails and accessible drawers.
Motion specialistBounded accepted-score effects, category signatures, reduced motion and quiet corrections/Undo.
Editorial / voiceIndependent settings; compare three licensed voices on identical facts/scripts with a capped authorized audition.
Exit gate:2/4/8-player layout and event fixtures pass; owner agrees feel and actual voice samples. No paid calls until the audition allowance is authorized; unrelated UI work can continue.
03

One score. One job. One audible line.

Prove the complete hosted-to-Android voice path early.

Backend / eventsDurable idempotent jobs, authenticated access, server keys, usage caps and crash/retry recovery.
Android / audioTrue streamed playback, first audible sample, focus/lifecycle/mute and host playback lease.
Editorial / contractsGrounding/completeness checks, captions, freshness/profile validity and exposure acknowledgments.
Exit gate: identified Android demonstrates event→job→caption→speech, no duplicate host playback and scoring survives provider failure. Measure actual cold/warm latency and cost; proposed speed targets remain experiments.
04

Give the game a story.

Easy invitations, understandable history and fresh commentary across repeat games.

History / invitationsUUID-owned chronology, corrected recap, two tournament standings and exact-table sign-in/verification return.
Commentary / memoryWhole-game significance and silence, authorized callbacks, premise cooldowns and audience boundaries.
UX / settingsCaptions/recent remarks, optional feedback, independent language/intensity/frequency and forget-history controls.
Exit gate: history reconciles; invitations preserve membership; private callbacks stay private; unheard lines never become shared memories. User-approved retention required before durable personal-memory collection.
05

Make a complete night resilient.

Scoring, celebrations and comedy coexist naturally through interruptions.

Multiplayer reliabilityRapid/racing scores, stale Undo, ties, final chains, completion reopening, network retry and return.
Audio / editorialWhole-game listening, novelty/fatigue, immediate mute/profanity change, no stale backlog and speaker/Bluetooth focus.
UX / performanceReal finger/VoiceOver checks,2/4/8 players, reduced motion, bounded effects and SFX ducking under speech.
Exit gate: integrated candidate passes configured Node24/native/backend qualification. Material authority, score-loss, privacy and incompatible-audio defects closed. Record actual limits and listening results.
06

Freeze, trial and accept the POC.

A real Android/iPhone game night, with evidence and an explicit owner decision.

Device / journeyExact builds on Android host and iPhone Safari/Home Screen; full games, rematches and next-day partial tournament return.
Audio / qualityRepeated real listening; relevance, naturalness, novelty, shock appropriateness, fatigue, latency and billed usage.
Reliability / evidenceAdverse cases, update/rollback, source/build/model/voice identities, support limits and privacy/reset checks.
Exit gate: product owner accepts bounded G3 software POC against agreed criteria. No automatic pass because six sprints elapsed. Commercial, hardware and deployment approvals remain separate.

How the agents work together

The lead remains the integration owner, with three specialists active per wave. Roles rotate as the work moves from UI/data contracts to streaming and qualification.

BoundaryWorking agreement
Source & file ownershipReconcile the intended dirty-tree baseline first. Isolated worktrees thereafter; one owner per file. Lead integrates shared prototype/UI/handler/bootstrap changes sequentially.
Accepted eventsPure rules and server authority stay intact. Visuals consume confirmed changes; durable commentary jobs run asynchronously outside score transactions.
Anonymous labelsDisplay labels never replace account UUIDs. Proposed shared captions/speech use stable Player A/B/C, rather than listener-dependent “You.” Confirm final voice/alias policy before integration.
Audio & memoryHost playback default, remote audio opt-in, shared captions. Profile changes invalidate incompatible content; callbacks require authorized exposure. Approved retention/delete policy before persistence.
EvidenceEvery agent returns assumptions, exact changes, meaningful checks and limits. Agent review is development review; phones and listening need actual observation.

Keep the POC bounded

Included

Manual hosted games/tournaments, comfortable smartphone score entry, anonymous opponent comparisons, premium effects, trustworthy return/Undo/update, category chronology/recap, invitation return, Android live commentary, shared captions, settings and bounded cross-game memory.

Stretch or later

Manual combination logging, broad analytics and keepsake exports are stretch work. Camera recognition, BLE/custom dice, Pi integration, manufacturing, automatic retraining, microphone/laughter detection and commercial release stay outside this POC.

Decisions and authorizations still needed

Final motion/voice defaults; capped paid voice allowance; retention/deletion and anonymous narration policy; accepted latency/listening criteria; real participants/devices; task-specific deployment/install approval. Sprint cadence is set after baseline/risk inventory, not guessed from the sprint count.

Recommended start: agree the updated touch prototype and the scoped POC criteria, then begin Sprint1. Prove the first real streamed Android voice in Sprint3 before committing to the complete repeated-game listening experience.
