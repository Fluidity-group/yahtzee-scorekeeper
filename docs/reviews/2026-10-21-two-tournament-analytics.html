What needs new instrumentation?

Manual scoring has no per-roll observations, and most turn-start timestamps are generated when the score is submitted. Foreground/background and reconnect events are not retained as a complete session history in these game records. Add lightweight session markers separately from scoring if exact play-session or turn timing becomes useful. Missing rolls must never be counted as failed combinations.

The next release should add the timing/category/opening cards to existing game and tournament insights first, then an account-based overall profile. This review is measured evidence and a design proposal, not a deployed analytics change.
