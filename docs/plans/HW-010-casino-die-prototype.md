## Design and impact

Revision A uses a small BLE module to reduce RF integration risk. Direct BLE to the app; case hub is not needed to solve repeated selection. Charging uses five identical transmitter channels. PCB generation/checks use isolated KiCad tooling. Package must distinguish design-review outputs from released fabrication files.

The LTC4124 temperature-monitored battery mode has materially higher current than HW-009's hypothetical 5 uA whole-die sleep scenario. Recalculate runtime from the selected circuit. LIS2DW12 SA0 must not be tied low without accounting for its internal pull-up; use high address selection for low standby drain.
